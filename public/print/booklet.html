About the house

Travel, held
in one place.

Tuwafute Machozi Tours is a Zanzibar house for private travel — island excursions, beach days, mainland safari, stays and transfers, arranged as one itinerary.

In Kiswahili, tuwafute machozi means let us wipe their tears. Guests who travel with us enable charity work across the island. Every trip includes a private guide. Prices are confirmed for your dates — nothing is taken on the website.

Mnemba Island · North Zanzibar

40%of tour revenue to charity

Through the Tuwafute Machozi Foundation, established 1 June 2023, forty percent of tour revenue is directed to community work in Zanzibar.

Twenty-eight named excursions

Discover
Zanzibar

Four coasts. One private guide. The catalog is the island itself — beaches, reefs, caves, spice, forest and Stone Town.

North

Nungwi and Kendwa for pale sand and evening light. Mnemba for the reef. The turtle sanctuary at the northern tip.

East

Paje and Jambiani for kite and lagoon. Kuza Cave. The Rock, set on its own stone in the sea.

South

Kizimkazi for the old mosque and the dolphin coast. Menai Bay, Pungume sandbank, and Maalum Cave.

West & Central

Stone Town, spice farms, Prison Island, Forodhani, Jozani Forest, mangroves, and the sunset dhow.
